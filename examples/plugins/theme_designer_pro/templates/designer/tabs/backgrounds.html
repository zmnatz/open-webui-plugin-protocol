Open WebUI Backgrounds
Off: these images cover the animation, more of it the higher their opacity. On: the animation stays on top of them at any opacity.
This mode has no Canvas FX animation, so the images stay in front of the theme here.
Dimming 100%
Open WebUI lays a dimming layer over these images, and the sign-in page picture gets the same one. At 100% it stays as Open WebUI draws it. Lower thins it to brighten them, until at 0% they show clear.
